📊 **U.S. Macroeconomic Outlook 2026–27 | Power BI Dashboard & Economic Analysis**

I’m excited to share my latest **Data Analytics & Power BI project**, focused on analyzing the U.S. economy using macroeconomic data from **2015–2025**.

As part of this project, I created:

🔹 An interactive **Power BI Dashboard (.pbix)**  
🔹 A detailed **U.S. Macroeconomic Outlook 2026–27 Word Report**  
🔹 KPI cards with year-on-year comparisons  
🔹 Historical trend analysis  
🔹 Economic and business-impact analysis  

The dashboard provides an executive-level view across seven key areas:

• Economic Growth & Demand  
• Inflation & Prices  
• Fiscal Health  
• Structural Risk  
• External Sector  
• Monetary & Financial Conditions  
• Labor Market  

I analyzed key indicators including **Real GDP Growth, Core PCE Inflation, Federal Funds Rate, Government Debt/GDP, Unemployment Rate, Consumption Growth, Labor Productivity, Private Investment, Fiscal Balance, Current Account, Financial Conditions, Real Wage Growth, and Job Openings**.

For the analysis, I referred to reliable sources including **BEA, BLS, Federal Reserve/FRED, U.S. Treasury, World Bank, and IMF**, depending on the indicator.

The main objective of this project was not only to visualize the data, but also to understand **what changed, why it changed, how different macroeconomic indicators are connected, and what these movements could mean for the economy and businesses**.

This project helped me strengthen my skills in:

**Power BI | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Visualization | Dashboard Design | Macroeconomic Analysis | Business Insights**
