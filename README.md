# USA-Macroeconomic-Dashboard-Executive-Overview-2015-2025
Excited to share my latest Power BI project — **USA Macroeconomic Dashboard | Executive Overview (2015–2025)** 🇺🇸📊

I developed this dashboard to analyze the U.S. economy through a single executive-level view, using historical macroeconomic indicators from 2015 to 2025.

The dashboard covers key areas including:

• Economic Growth & Demand  
• Inflation & Prices  
• Fiscal Health  
• Structural Risk  
• External Sector  
• Monetary & Financial Conditions  
• Labor Market  

Key indicators analyzed include Real GDP Growth, Core PCE, Federal Funds Rate, Government Debt/GDP, Unemployment Rate, consumption, productivity, private investment, fiscal balance, current account, financial conditions, real wages, and job openings.

For the project, I worked with data from reliable sources such as **BEA, BLS, Federal Reserve/FRED, U.S. Treasury, World Bank and IMF**, depending on the indicator.

The main objective was not only to visualize the data, but also to understand how changes in **growth, inflation, interest rates, employment, fiscal conditions and financial markets** are connected.

This project helped me strengthen my skills in **Power BI, data cleaning, DAX, data visualization, economic analysis and dashboard design**.
